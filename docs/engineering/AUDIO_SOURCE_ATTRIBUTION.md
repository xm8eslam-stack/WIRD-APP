# WIRD — Quran Recitation Audio Attribution & Rights

## Current source

WIRD currently streams verse-by-verse MP3 files directly from EveryAyah:

- Host: https://everyayah.com/
- URL pattern: `https://everyayah.com/data/{reciter-folder}/{SSSAAA}.mp3`
- Audio is streamed by the browser; WIRD does not bundle, mirror, or re-host the MP3 files.
- The selected reciter is shown in the player, and the player links back to EveryAyah.

## Rights status — do not overstate

The Quran text license (Tanzil CC BY 3.0) does **not** apply to recitation recordings. A public survey of EveryAyah's published materials reports that EveryAyah has no site-wide audio license and that recordings remain the rights of their reciters/publishers. See the Maqra survey and rights notice: https://github.com/Abdalla-Eldoumani/maqra and https://github.com/Abdalla-Eldoumani/maqra/blob/main/RIGHTS.md.

Until a reciter-specific permission or license is confirmed:

1. Keep clear attribution to the named reciter and a visible link to EveryAyah.
2. Do not claim the audio is CC BY, public domain, or generally redistributable.
3. Do not download, bundle, mirror, repackage, or redistribute recordings.
4. Treat the current use as non-commercial streaming only; verify the relevant recording's terms before any commercial use or redistribution.
5. Respond to rights-holder removal or correction requests promptly.

The playback-speed control changes the browser's local playback rate only. It does not modify, export, or redistribute the source MP3.

## Release gate

The source/rights limitation must remain visible in release documentation. A future audio-host migration or offline-audio feature requires a separate rights review for every reciter and recording set.
